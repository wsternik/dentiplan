## Notes

The front page at `/` is the `ui-redesign` landing from 2026-09-05: a headline, two
paragraphs and a miniature of the two variant tabs. It follows the design brief but
reads as a stub. It does not tell a stranger who the product is for, the forced line
break in the headline strands a word on desktop, the sample is too small to be the
hero the brief asked for, and there is no header or footer, so the page ends
mid-air.

This change replaces the page with a single, quiet screen whose one memorable
device is the product itself: a dentist's messy diagnosis note on the left, the
patient page it turns into on the right. One action on the whole page: sign in.
No sign-up, no second CTA, no new colours, no new dependencies.

Visual system is unchanged. Tokens, both typefaces, the squared tab with a grip
edge and the "colour means something clinical" rule all come from
`context/archive/2026-09-05-ui-redesign/design-brief.md`; this change spends them
better on one page.

Plan: `plan.md` next to this file.
